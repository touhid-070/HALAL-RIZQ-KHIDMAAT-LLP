Understanding Halal

Halal (permitted)

Lawful for Muslims to eat and use. This includes vegetables, fruits, grains, milk, eggs, fish and meat from permitted animals slaughtered according to Islamic rules.

Haram (forbidden)

Not permitted. This includes pork, alcohol, blood, carrion, animals not slaughtered according to Islamic rules, and anything made from or contaminated with them.

Mashbooh (doubtful)

Of unclear status. Ingredients such as gelatine, some emulsifiers, enzymes and flavourings may come from animal or alcohol sources, so their origin must be checked before approval.

Who can be certified

Product Manufacturer

Factories and brands that make packaged food, beverages, ingredients or other consumer products.

Food Premises

Restaurants, cafes, caterers, bakeries and central kitchens.

Outlet / Hotel

Individual outlets, franchises and hotel kitchens.

Abattoir

Slaughter facilities for meat and poultry, checked against Halal slaughter norms.
