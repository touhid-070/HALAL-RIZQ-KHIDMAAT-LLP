About Halal Rizq Khidmaat LLP

Who we are

Halal Rizq Khidmaat LLP is a Halal certification service. We work with restaurants, manufacturers, hotels and slaughterhouses to check that their products and processes meet Halal requirements, and we give consumers a simple way to confirm that a certificate is genuine.

Our vision

A marketplace where every consumer can trust the Halal label on what they buy.

Our mission

To certify businesses fairly, audit them thoroughly and make every certificate easy to verify.

Our aims

Spread awareness of Halal and Haram, help businesses meet Halal standards, and support exporters who need certification for Muslim markets.

Impartiality

Decisions are made on the facts of the audit only, with no pressure from commercial or other interests.

Confidentiality

Your formulas, supplier lists and business records stay private and are used only for certification.

Transparency

Every certificate has a number, Sr. No. and QR code. Revoked or expired certificates show clearly on the verification page.

Our certification process

  1. You register and submit an application with your business and product details.
  2. Our team reviews the application and asks for any missing documents.
  3. Auditors visit your premises to check ingredients, processes, hygiene and storage.
  4. If everything meets the standard, we issue your certificate, valid for the period shown on it.
  5. Certificates can be withdrawn at any time if the standard is not maintained.
